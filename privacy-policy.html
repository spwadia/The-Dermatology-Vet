Privacy Policy

The Dermatology Vet

1. Introduction

Welcome to the website of The Dermatology Vet, a specialist veterinary skin, ear and allergy service. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal data while you interact with our website.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information: We may gather personal details such as your name, phone number, email address, and details of your pet’s condition when you fill out forms, refer a case, or contact us through our website.

Usage Data: Our site automatically collects usage data like IP addresses, browser types, and pages visited to enhance your user experience and improve our website.

3. Use of Collected Information

We utilize the collected data for the following:

  • To respond to your queries and arrange appointments, tele-consults and referrals.
  • To stay in touch with you about your pet’s care.
  • To improve our website’s content and features.

4. Information Sharing

We neither sell nor trade or rent your personal information to external parties. However, we might share your data under specific conditions:

  • With your own veterinarian, as part of your pet’s care.
  • With trusted third parties who assist us in managing our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as court orders or subpoenas.
  • To protect our rights, privacy, and safety, as well as those of our users.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security protocols to protect your personal data from unauthorized access or modification. However, please note that no online transaction is entirely secure.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website employs cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience and collect usage data. You can manage these through your browser settings.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to other third-party sites. We are not liable for their privacy policies, and we recommend that you review their privacy statements.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for users who are above the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Your Choices

  • Update or remove your personal data by contacting us.
  • Control cookies via your browser settings.

10. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. All changes will be published here, and the “Last Updated” timestamp will be modified accordingly.

11. Contact Us

For any questions or concerns regarding your privacy and data, feel free to contact us at hello@thedermatology.vet.
