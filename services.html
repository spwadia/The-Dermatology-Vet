Our Services

Referral dermatology for every species we are asked to see — and an honest answer about which cases genuinely need a specialist.

Consultation & Diagnosis

A full dermatological work-up: history, lesion mapping, skin scrapes, tape cytology, trichogram, Wood’s lamp, fungal culture and biopsy where indicated.

Read More

Allergy Testing & Diet Trials

Serum IgE allergy testing, intradermal testing where indicated, and properly supervised eight-week elimination diet trials — run to protocol, not guesswork.

Read More

The Chronic Ear

Video-otoscopy, deep ear flushing under general anaesthesia, myringotomy and middle-ear sampling for the ear that keeps coming back.

Read More

Equine Dermatology

Visits to stud farms, riding schools and private yards for sweet itch, sarcoids, pastern dermatitis and non-healing wounds.

Exotics & Avian

Feather-destructive disorders, scaly face in budgerigars, dysecdysis in reptiles and pododermatitis in rabbits and guinea pigs.
