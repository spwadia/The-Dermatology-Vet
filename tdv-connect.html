Send a case

Three steps, two working days

  1. 1

    Send the case

    Use the form below or email refer@thedermatology.vet. A few lines of history and good photographs are enough to start; cytology images and lab results help.

  2. 2

    Get a written plan

    Within two working days: what we think it is, the next test worth doing, and a treatment plan with doses.

  3. 3

    Keep the case

    You carry on treating. If the animal needs to be seen, Dr Awale sees it in Pune and sends it back to you with a full report.
