Equine Dermatology

Visits to stud farms, riding schools and private yards for sweet itch, sarcoids, pastern dermatitis and non-healing wounds.

Horses are seen where they live. We visit stud farms, riding schools, racing yards and private stables across Pune and the surrounding district.

What we see most

  • Insect bite hypersensitivity (sweet itch) — the mane-and-tail itch that returns every monsoon, managed with barrier strategy, rug and stabling changes and medication.
  • Sarcoids — assessment, biopsy where it is safe to take one, and a treatment plan that accounts for site, type and the horse’s job.
  • Pastern dermatitis — the chronically scabbed heel, which is a pattern and not a diagnosis; the work is in finding the cause underneath.
  • Non-healing wounds and exuberant granulation tissue.
  • Dermatophytosis and rain scald in yards with a herd problem rather than an individual one.

Yard visits can be arranged for several horses in one attendance, which is usually the sensible way to handle a herd outbreak. Your own veterinarian stays in charge of the case throughout.

Referring a case? Send history, photographs and any cytology through TDV Connect and you will have a written opinion within two working days.
