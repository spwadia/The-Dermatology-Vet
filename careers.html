Careers

Building India’s first specialist veterinary dermatology service — which means building the team as well as the clinic.

Why here

There is no established veterinary dermatology specialty in India. That is the difficulty and it is also the opportunity: the clinicians who join now will set the standard rather than inherit it.

Who we are looking for

  • Veterinary dermatologists — board-certified, in training, or with substantial dermatology caseload experience.
  • Residents — through the group’s academy, under specialist supervision.
  • Interns and general practitioners wanting to build a dermatology caseload.
  • Veterinary nurses and technicians — dermatology nursing is a specialty of its own: cytology, ear procedures, diet-trial coaching and long-term case follow-up.
  • Client care and practice management for the Pune clinic.

What we offer

  • Structured education through the group’s academy, and support for external certification.
  • A specialist caseload from day one, fed by the group’s referral network.
  • Colleagues in ophthalmology, oncology, emergency, radiology, pathology and behaviour.

No vacancy that fits? Write to us anyway. Send a CV and a note about the kind of dermatology you want to do to careers@thedermatology.vet.
