Co-founder of The Dermatology Vet

Dr Madhavi Awale

BVSc & AH · MVSc · Dermatology training, ESAVS

Dr Madhavi Awale co-founded The Dermatology Vet to give India the specialist veterinary skin service it has never had.

She qualified from Bombay Veterinary College in 2010, took a master’s degree in veterinary pharmacology, and trained in small animal dermatology with the European School for Advanced Veterinary Studies (ESAVS). Since 2016 she has run Pet Wellness Clinic and Derm Centre in Pimple Saudagar, Pune, a practice built around skin disease in dogs and cats.

She also teaches other vets, including in the IAVD dermatology webinar series. At The Dermatology Vet she sees referred cases in Pune and reviews the cases that vets across India send through TDV Connect.

The team around her

We are recruiting the veterinarians and dermatology nurses who will work alongside Dr Awale in Pune. Dermatology nursing is a skill of its own — cytology, ear procedures, diet-trial coaching and long-term follow-up — and we train for it. If that could be you, see Careers.

How the clinical standard is held

The Dermatology Vet is one of the specialist businesses of Veterinary Holdings International. Clinical standards are set by the group’s Dermatology Specialty Council rather than by any individual clinic.

Working with the rest of the group

Skin disease does not stay in the skin. Cases are seen jointly with sister practices where that is what the animal needs — The Eye Vet, which shares our premises in Pune, for eyelid and periocular disease; The Cancer Vet for skin tumours; The Exotics Vet for birds, reptiles and small mammals; and The Behaviour Vet where self-trauma or feather-destructive behaviour has a behavioural component.

The Dermatology Vet, your specialist partner in skin, ear and allergy care for dogs, cats, horses, birds and exotic companion animals.
