Finding us

NECO Alpha is in Ingawale Nagar, Pimple Nilakh, in Pimpri-Chinchwad. Take the lift to the 5th floor. We share the premises with The Eye Vet, our sister ophthalmology practice, so if you have visited The Eye Vet in Pune you already know the way.

Two specialist teams under one roof

Skin disease often reaches the eyelids and the skin around the eyes. Because we work in the same premises, the dermatology and ophthalmology teams can see those cases together, without a second journey.

Appointments

Every appointment is booked in advance. Request an appointment online or email us at hello@thedermatology.vet. Please bring previous test results and the packaging of every medication, shampoo and food your animal is on.

Coming from outside Pune

Start with a tele-consult. If you are a veterinarian, send the case through TDV Connect and you will have a written opinion within two working days. If the animal needs to be seen in person, we will tell you, and plan the visit so that it takes as few trips as possible.
