Allergy Testing & Diet Trials

Serum IgE allergy testing, intradermal testing where indicated, and properly supervised eight-week elimination diet trials — run to protocol, not guesswork.

There is no blood test that diagnoses a food allergy, and no allergy test that diagnoses atopic dermatitis. Both are clinical diagnoses — reached by excluding everything else first.

The elimination diet trial

An adverse food reaction is diagnosed one way only: a strict elimination diet for eight weeks, followed by a provocation challenge. A trial that runs six weeks, or that allows a single treat, a flavoured tablet or a chew, tells you nothing at all — which is why most diet trials fail before they begin.

We set the trial up properly: a novel or hydrolysed protein chosen around what the animal has already eaten, written instructions for everyone in the household, a strategy for medication that does not break the trial, and a scheduled check at weeks four and eight.

Allergy testing, and what it is actually for

Serum IgE testing and intradermal testing do not tell you whether an animal is allergic. They tell you what to put in an immunotherapy vaccine once the clinical diagnosis of atopic dermatitis has already been made. Used in that order they are valuable; used as a screening test they generate false positives and wasted money.

On immunotherapy in India. Allergen-specific immunotherapy (ASIT) is not currently manufactured or licensed in India and we do not promise it. Where it is clinically indicated we will say so and discuss the import position honestly. Everything else in the allergy plan — parasite control, infection control, barrier care, diet and modern anti-pruritic medication — is available here today.

Long-term control

For most atopic animals the realistic goal is comfortable skin on the lowest effective treatment, reviewed twice a year. Oclacitinib is available in India both as the originator and as generics, which makes long-term control affordable for a far wider range of households than in most markets.
