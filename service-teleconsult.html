Tele-dermatology

TDV Connect — a written specialist opinion within two working days for referring veterinarians anywhere in India, from history and photographs.

TDV Connect is a specialist dermatology opinion for veterinarians, delivered without the client having to travel.

How it works

  1. You send the history, your photographs and any cytology images through the referral form.
  2. A dermatologist reviews the case and replies within two working days — usually sooner.
  3. You receive a written opinion: a differential list, the next diagnostic step, a treatment plan with doses, and a clear statement of when the case should be seen in person instead.

The case stays yours. We do not contact your client, and we do not take the case on unless you ask us to. Where a physical referral is the right answer we will say so rather than manage a case badly at a distance.

For pet owners. A tele-consult is also available to you directly if you live outside Pune — but a first dermatology opinion is almost always better in person, because cytology cannot be done down a camera. We will tell you honestly which one your animal needs.

What it costs

Tele-dermatology is charged per case, not per message, and a follow-up question on the same case within thirty days is included. Current fees are confirmed when you submit the form.
